# Legal AI Chatbot - Questions You Can Ask 💬

## 📋 Case Information Questions

### Basic Case Details
- "What is this case about?"
- "What is the case number?"
- "Who is the petitioner/claimant?"
- "When did the accident happen?"
- "Where did the accident occur?"
- "What is the FIR number?"
- "Which tribunal is handling this case?"

### Accident Details
- "How did the accident happen?"
- "What was the accident location?"
- "What time did the accident occur?"
- "What vehicle was involved?"
- "Who was driving the vehicle?"
- "Were there any witnesses?"

## 🎯 Smart Questions (AI Understands Context)

### Follow-up Questions
After asking "What are the injuries?", you can ask:
- "Which one is the most severe?" (AI remembers previous question)
- "What was the treatment for that?" (AI knows you mean the severe injury)
- "How much compensation for that injury?"

### Conversational Questions
- "Explain that in simpler terms"
- "Give me more details"
- "Can you elaborate on that?"
- "What does that mean for our case?"

### Comparative Questions
- "Is this injury worse than the other one?"
- "Which hospital provided better treatment?"
- "Compare the treatment cost before and after surgery"

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

### Be Specific
❌ Bad: "Tell me about the case"
✅ Good: "What are the documented injuries and their severity?"

### Use Medical Terms When Available
✅ "What fractures are documented?"
✅ "What analgesics were prescribed?"
✅ "What is the Glasgow Coma Scale score?"

### Reference Exact Pages
✅ "What does page 7 say about the fracture?"
✅ "Summarize the discharge summary on page 15"

### Ask One Thing at a Time
❌ Bad: "Tell me about injuries, treatments, medications, and compensation"
✅ Good: "What are the injuries?" (then ask about treatments separately)

### Use Natural Language
✅ "How much money did hospital treatment cost?"
✅ "What pills were given for pain?"
✅ "When did the accident happen?"

## ⚠️ What the Chatbot CANNOT Answer

### Out of Scope Questions
❌ "What's the weather today?"
❌ "Tell me a joke"
❌ "What's 2+2?"
❌ "Who is the Prime Minister?"

### Questions Requiring External Data
❌ "What's the current interest rate for compensation?"
❌ "What's the latest Supreme Court judgment this week?"
❌ "What's the tribunal's current backlog?"

### Personal Legal Advice
❌ "Should I settle or go to trial?" (This needs lawyer's judgment)
❌ "What are my chances of winning?" (Needs complete case analysis by lawyer)

### Future Predictions
❌ "When will the case be decided?"
❌ "Will the judge rule in our favor?"

## 🎯 Example Conversation Flow

**You:** What are the main injuries?
**AI:** The patient sustained:
1. Compound fracture of right tibia
2. Head injury with brief loss of consciousness
3. Multiple lacerations on arms and legs
[Sources: Page 4 of Medical Records]

**You:** Which one is most severe?
**AI:** The compound fracture of right tibia is the most severe injury because:
- Required surgical intervention (ORIF)
- Led to 45-day hospitalization
- Resulted in 30% permanent disability
[Sources: Page 7 of Discharge Summary]

**You:** What was the treatment for that?
**AI:** Treatment for the tibial fracture included:
1. Emergency stabilization
2. ORIF surgery with plate fixation
3. Post-op antibiotics and pain management
4. 6 weeks of physiotherapy
[Sources: Page 8 of Surgical Notes]

**You:** Calculate compensation for this injury
**AI:** Compensation breakdown for tibial fracture:
- Medical expenses: ₹3,50,000
- Loss of income (90 days): ₹1,80,000
- Pain and suffering: ₹2,00,000
- Permanent disability (30%): ₹5,00,000
**Total: ₹12,30,000**
[Based on Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 Section 166]
